# 888box Logo Redesign — Concept Round 1

## Design brief

**Summary**: 888box is a fast, practical asset-sharing platform for images, videos, audio, and files. The mark should feel like one dependable place where many things are stored and shared.

**Audience**: Developers, small teams, creators, and self-hosters who meet the brand in a browser tab, upload center, PWA icon, README, and shared links.

**Brand adjectives**: precise, open, dependable, energetic, lightly playful.

**Avoid feeling**: generic cloud storage, enterprise-heavy, disposable tech trend.

**Promise / big idea**: One compact place for every asset.

**Competitive landscape**: Hosting and developer-tool marks often use containers, cubes, clouds, and blue/purple palettes. 888box keeps its orange equity and moves toward a more ownable geometry.

**Mark type hypothesis**: Combination mark built from a standalone geometric symbol plus a custom outlined wordmark. The symbol must stand on its own as a favicon and app icon.

**Must work on**: 16px favicon, 32px browser tab, 192/512px PWA icon, dark UI, light UI, one-colour print, README, and social avatar.

**Constraints**: Preserve the 888box name and orange family as a later colour direction. No live text, rasters, filters, or gradients in the final master SVG.

**Success criteria**: Clear silhouette at 16px; recognizable without the wordmark; visually distinct from literal package-box and cloud marks; balanced in black, white, and orange.

**Assumptions I made**:

- The product name remains `888box`.
- The strongest existing equity is the orange family and the repeated `888` rhythm.
- The current logo can evolve substantially in geometry while keeping those two anchors.
- The initial checkpoint should compare three symbol directions before building a full asset kit.

## Concept directions

### A — Open Stack

Three compact loops align as a modular stack. A deliberate opening at the upper-right edge turns the repeated 8 rhythm into a container that can accept many asset types.

### B — Link 8

One continuous ribbon creates three connected chambers. The shared center joins the three 8s and suggests a single pipeline from upload to share.

### C — Three Windows

Three rounded windows sit inside an open square. The mark reads as 888 at a glance, while the missing fourth window leaves a clear sense of openness and access.
