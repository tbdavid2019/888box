# 888 Box Logo Guidelines

## The idea

The Link 8 symbol joins four rounded chambers into one connected system. It carries the rhythm of `888` while leaving a small open junction that suggests access, upload, and sharing.

## Approved versions

- `888box-horizontal-color.svg`: primary lockup for light backgrounds.
- `888box-horizontal-dark.svg`: primary lockup for the dark 888box interface.
- `888box-stacked-color.svg`: square layouts and social profiles.
- `888box-symbol-orange.svg`: symbol-only use.
- `brand-mark.svg`: live-site symbol-only asset.
- `brand-lockup.svg`: live-site dark-background lockup.

## Clear space and minimum size

Use clear space equal to the height of one small chamber in the symbol on all sides.

| Version | Screen minimum | Print minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Horizontal lockup | 180px wide | 30mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 96px wide | 18mm wide |
| Symbol | 16px | 6mm |

Use the simplified icon tile for favicon and app-icon contexts.

Use orange on white, white on the dark interface, ink on white, or white on the orange icon tile. Keep the one-colour black and white versions available for print and constrained media.

## Typography

The logo wordmark is custom outlined geometry. Recreate it from the SVG paths; do not type it with a system font.

For surrounding UI, continue using the existing 888box font stack in `static/css/fonts.css`.

## Usage

- Keep the relative scale and spacing of the symbol and wordmark fixed.
- Keep the symbol upright and optically centred.
- Use the symbol alone only when the audience already knows 888 Box or when the available size is too small for the lockup.
- Use `brand-lockup.svg` on the current dark portal and the colour lockup on light documentation or print surfaces.

## Avoid

- Stretching, squashing, rotating, or recolouring the mark outside the palette.
- Adding shadows, outlines, gradients, or effects to the master artwork.
- Typing a replacement wordmark or rearranging the lockup parts.
- Placing the mark on a busy image without a calm container.

Trademark clearance remains a separate legal review. The design library was used for category research only; its marks were not traced.
